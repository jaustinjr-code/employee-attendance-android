# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information Employee Attendance ("the app") uses, why, where it is kept, and the choices you have. The short version: the app works on your device. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no servers of its own, and the developer never receives your information.

## Information the app uses

### Location

The app uses your device's location to tell when you arrive at or leave a worksite you have registered, so it can show whether you are on site and, if you allow it, clock you in and out automatically.

- Precise or approximate location while the app is open, if you grant location access.
- Background location ("Allow all the time"), only if you choose to grant it. This lets proximity tracking and automatic clock-in work while the app is closed. While it is active, Android shows a notification.

Location readings are used on your device to compare your position with your worksites. The app does not keep a history of where you have been. It stores only your most recent on-site or off-site state for each worksite.

### Worksites

When you register a worksite, the app stores the name you give it, its address, its coordinates, and the radius you choose.

### Attendance history

The app records each clock-in and clock-out: the worksite, the time, and whether it was automatic or manual. This history powers the attendance screen and your reports.

### Status updates

If status updates are turned on, the app asks a few short questions when you clock out. Your answers are kept so you can review and edit them later.

### Display name

If you set a display name on the account screen, it is used to greet you in the app.

### Settings

The app stores your choices, such as how automatic clock-in should notify you and whether address lookup is allowed.

## How information is stored

Everything listed above is stored only on your device, in encrypted storage. It is excluded from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, so it is not copied to a new phone or to your Google account.

## When information leaves your device

The app does not send your information to the developer, to your employer, or to anyone else on its own. Information leaves your device only in these cases:

- Address lookup. When you search for a worksite's address, or capture your current location while registering a worksite, the app uses your device's built-in geocoding service to turn an address into coordinates or coordinates into an address. On most Android devices this service is provided by Google, and the text or coordinates being looked up are sent to it. You can turn off looking up an address from your current location in Settings, under Privacy.
- Location services. The app relies on Google Play services on your device to provide location readings and to watch for arrival at and departure from your worksites. Google's handling of that information is governed by Google's own privacy policy and your device's location settings.
- Reports you share. When you share an attendance report, the app creates a text file and hands it to the app you pick, such as email or a cloud drive. Nothing is sent until you choose a destination and send it. Once shared, the report is handled by that app and by whoever receives it, including your employer if you send it to them.

The app does not sell your information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app uses them to confirm or undo automatic clock-ins and clock-outs, to prompt status updates, to show that background tracking is active, and to deliver your biweekly report summary. Notification content is generated on your device.

## Your choices

- Permissions. You can grant, limit, or revoke location and notification access at any time in Android settings. The app keeps working with reduced features when permissions are denied.
- Address lookup. You can turn off looking up an address from your current location in Settings.
- Status updates and reports. You can turn status update prompts and the biweekly report on or off in Settings.
- Editing. You can remove individual worksites, and you can review and edit past status updates.
- Deleting. Settings > Your data > Delete all data permanently removes every worksite, all attendance history, and all status updates from your device. Uninstalling the app removes everything it has stored, including your settings and display name.

## How long information is kept

Information stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Because the developer never receives it, there is no copy elsewhere to delete.

## Children

The app is a workplace tool and is not directed to children under 13. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in the app with a new effective date. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy or how the app handles your information, contact the developer through the app's store listing.
